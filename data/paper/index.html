Columns

sector
The market the row belongs to.
metric
What is measured, in words.
entity
The company, group or total the figure is for.
year
The date or period, as the source states it. Indian financial years run April to March and are written 2023-24.
value, unit
The figure and its unit.
source_title, publisher, url, locator
The document, who published it, where it is, and the page, table or paragraph.
note
Definitions, traps and whether the figure is derived.

The figures each chart draws are also in series.csv. The paper and its pages are written from these tables by scripts/build_paper.py in the repository, so a corrected row changes every page that uses it.
