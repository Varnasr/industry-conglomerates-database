Findings · City gas · Part 1, Concentration

Adani Total Gas supplies 6% of India's piped household gas connections and runs 8% of its CNG stations

Adani Total Gas holds licences for 34 of the geographical areas the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board has authorised, and its 50:50 joint venture with Indian Oil holds 19 more. The joint venture adds 1.5% of household connections and 4.7% of CNG stations; it is kept separate here because Indian Oil owns half of it.

These shares have barely moved since 2020-21, with household connections between 6.2% and 6.3%, CNG stations between 6.6% and 7.9%. City gas is a sizeable business for the group without being a dominant one.

How this was measured

Area-by-area tables of the gas regulator, as reprinted in the Petroleum Planning and Analysis Cell's Ready Reckoner, summed by company over the printed national totals. The regulator's own site could not be reached, and no table was found for March 2016 to 2019, 2024 or 2025. The April 2026 table's rows add to about 2% more than its printed total, so the 2026 shares are indicative.
