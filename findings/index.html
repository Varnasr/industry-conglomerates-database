Findings · Part 1, Concentration

What the official figures show

Each page takes one finding from the paper, shows it in a chart, and says how it was measured. Every number comes from a government, regulator or company document, listed under the chart with the page it came from.

  1. Ports

    Adani's ports handled 27% of India's port cargo in 2025-26, up from 12% in 2013-14

  2. Airports

    The airports Adani runs carry 23% of India's air passengers, fewer than the 28% the same airports carried in 2013-14

  3. Coal power

    Adani Power runs 24% of India's private coal power capacity, mostly bought in a market that stopped growing

  4. Renewable power

    Adani Green's share of India's renewable capacity has tripled since 2017-18, to 8.6%

  5. Transmission

    Adani is building or running 27 of 176 competitively bid transmission projects, and owns under 4% of the grid

  6. Telecom

    Jio holds 41% of India's mobile subscribers ten years after it launched, and the market is now highly concentrated

  7. Telecom

    Jio earns 45% of India's mobile and access-service revenue, and Jio and Airtel together 84%

  8. Refining

    Reliance's share of India's refining capacity has fallen from 35% in 2009 to 26%

  9. Cement

    The Adani group built India's second largest cement business in four years, almost entirely by acquisition

  10. City gas

    Adani Total Gas supplies 6% of India's piped household gas connections and runs 8% of its CNG stations

  11. Economic weight

    The listed Adani companies went from 1% to 5% of India's stock market value in two years, and fell back after January 2023
