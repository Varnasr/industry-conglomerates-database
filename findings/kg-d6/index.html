Findings · Oil and gas · Part 2, How the assets were obtained

The auditor could not validate Reliance's KG-D6 costs, and later put excess cost recovery at US$ 1,548 million

The Comptroller and Auditor General's 2011 audit found that the whole KG-D6 block had been treated as a discovery area, so none was relinquished; that estimated development spending rose from US$ 2.4 billion to US$ 8.8 billion; and that 10 (8 to Aker Group) high-value contracts went on single financial bids. The 2016 follow-up put excess cost recovery at US$ 1,547.85 million.

The auditor did not find that costs were inflated; it said it could not validate them. Separately, the arbitral award rejecting the government's gas-migration claim was set aside by the Delhi High Court in February 2025, and the petroleum ministry has demanded US$ 2.81 billion.
