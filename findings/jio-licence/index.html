Findings · Telecom · Part 2, How the assets were obtained

The federal auditor found that Jio's 2013 move to a voice licence gave it a Rs 3,367 crore advantage

Infotel, which became Reliance Jio, won broadband spectrum in all 22 circles in 2010 as an internet licensee. In 2013 it moved to a unified licence permitting voice calls by paying Rs 1,658 crore, the fee set in 2001.

The Comptroller and Auditor General's Report No. 20 of 2015 found that the fee's value in 2013 was higher, that the company got an "undue advantage" of Rs 3,367 crore, and rejected the telecom department's explanation.
