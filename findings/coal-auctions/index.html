Findings · Coal · Part 2, Routes to the state

Four of the six coal auctions Adani won with a published bidder count drew two bidders or fewer

Adani companies won 8 mines in the Ministry of Coal's commercial auctions between 2020 and 2023. The ministry published the number of bidders for 6 of them: 1, 2, 2, 2, 4, 6. Gondbahera Ujheni East went to an Adani company on a single bid at the second attempt, with the approval of the Empowered Committee of Secretaries.

In one auction two Adani companies qualified for the same mine, which another bidder won. The ministry has said that no link between another bidder, Cavill Mining, and Adani has been established.

The documents

Bidder counts and winning offers from the Ministry of Coal's and the Nominated Authority's auction documents and press releases; ownership of the winning companies from Adani Enterprises' related-party disclosure to the BSE. Two auctions have no published bidder count and are left out of the count.
