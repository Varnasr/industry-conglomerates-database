Dharavi went to Adani at Rs 5,069 crore after a first tender with a higher Rs 7,200 crore bid was cancelled

In the first Dharavi tender, opened in January 2019, Seclink bid Rs 7,200 crore and Adani Rs 4,529 crore. Maharashtra cancelled it in 2020, on the ground that railway land had not been part of the bid. In the fresh tender of 2022, 3, and Adani was selected at Rs 5,069 crore.

The Bombay High Court dismissed Seclink's challenge in December 2024. It recorded Adani's argument that the two price bids measured different things, because the bid is the money a bidder commits to the project company.
