Findings · Airports · Part 2, Routes to the state

Adani won all six airports leased in 2018-19, in a round where the finance ministry and NITI Aayog had questioned the bid documents

The rules for the round, set by an Empowered Group of Secretaries in November 2018, put no cap on how many airports one bidder could win and required no airport experience. Adani Enterprises quoted the highest fee per passenger at all six.

Notes from the Department of Economic Affairs and NITI Aayog on 10 December 2018 asked for the bid terms to be reviewed and said the PPP guidelines' two-stage process had not been followed. The Kerala High Court held that the committees could overrule such notes and had considered them, and dismissed Kerala's challenge.

The documents

From the Kerala High Court's judgment of 19 October 2020 in W.P.(C) 2224 of 2019 and connected cases (paras 13, 31, 41-54), the Ministry of Civil Aviation's press note of 21 January 2021, the Cabinet decisions, and Lok Sabha answers of 2019 and 2024. The notes themselves and the per-passenger bids at five of the six airports were not found in any public record.
