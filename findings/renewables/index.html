Findings · Renewable power · Part 1, Concentration

Adani Green's share of India's renewable capacity has tripled since 2017-18, to 8.6%

Adani Green ran 1,958 MW in 2017-18 and 19,294 MW in 2025-26, while renewable capacity excluding large hydro across India went from 69,022 MW to 223,273 MW.

The share is of a market growing very fast, so it is the clearest case in this paper of a group gaining share by building. Adani Green's larger figures, such as a 'locked-in portfolio', count capacity contracted but not running, and are not used.

How this was measured

Operational capacity in MW (AC) at 31 March from Adani Green's annual reports, including its joint ventures with TotalEnergies in full. The denominator is renewable capacity excluding large hydro in the CEA and MNRE figures, which mix rooftop and off-grid capacity and do not always say whether they count AC or DC; the share is approximate to that extent. In the March 2025 report the renewables total is as of 31 January.

Every figure above is in the data tables with its source and page. Read the argument in full in Part 1 of the paper.
