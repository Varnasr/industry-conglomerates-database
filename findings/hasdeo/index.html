Findings · Coal · Part 2, Routes to the state

A forest committee rejected the Hasdeo coal clearance in June 2011; the minister overruled it the next day

The environment ministry's Forest Advisory Committee recommended on 22 June 2011 that 1,898 hectares of forest in Hasdeo Arand should not be diverted for the Parsa East and Kanta Basan blocks, which the Rajasthan state generation company holds and an Adani company mines. On 23 June the Minister of State for Environment granted first-stage clearance, giving six reasons.

In March 2014 the National Green Tribunal sent the clearance back to the committee and suspended work. Permissions for the second phase were granted in 2022 and are under challenge in the Supreme Court and the Chhattisgarh High Court.

The documents

From the National Green Tribunal's judgment in Appeal 73 of 2012 (24 March 2014), which sets out the committee's recommendation and the minister's order, and the Tribunal's order of 28 May 2025. The reported Supreme Court stay of the Tribunal's suspension and the Chhattisgarh Assembly's 2022 resolution rest on press reports and are labelled as such.
