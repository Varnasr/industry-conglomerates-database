Findings · Debt · Part 3, Finance and risk

Adani Green's net debt is 8.3 times a year's earnings and Adani Enterprises' 6.0; Adani Power and Adani Ports are under two

Net debt as a multiple of a year's operating earnings differs widely across the Adani companies. In 2025-26 it was 8.32 at Adani Green, 6.04 at Adani Enterprises, 4.35 at Adani Energy Solutions, 1.93 at Adani Power and 1.82 at Adani Ports. Reliance Industries was at 0.60.

Adani Power's fall from about eight times in 2016-17 is the largest change. The companies that are building fastest carry the most debt.

How this was measured

Consolidated borrowings, cash, bank balances and current investments, and earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, from annual reports filed with the NSE (to 2021-22) and audited results (from 2022-23). Reliance uses the company's own net debt and EBITDA. A group total is not given because the companies' debts overlap.
