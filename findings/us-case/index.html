Findings · Courts · Part 3, Finance and risk

The US fraud charges against the Adanis were dismissed without a finding on the merits, and the SEC case ended in a settlement without admission

The indictment unsealed in November 2024 charged Gautam Adani, Sagar Adani and Vneet Jaain with securities and wire fraud, and five others with bribery conspiracy. On 10 August 2026 the court dismissed the charges against the three, on the prosecutors' motion, with prejudice, stating that this expressed no opinion about the merits.

The same day it entered a consent judgment in the SEC's civil case, under which Gautam Adani pays US$ 6 million and Sagar Adani US$ 12 million, without admitting or denying the allegations. The bribery charges against the five others remain before the court.
