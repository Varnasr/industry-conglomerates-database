Findings · Solar power · Part 2, How the assets were obtained

One package of the 2019 solar tender drew no bids, and only Adani Green took the 4,000 MW moved from it

The Solar Energy Corporation of India's tender of June 2019 linked 8,000 MW of solar power to building solar factories. The Central Electricity Regulatory Commission's order records that 3, and that only Adani Green accepted the capacity moved from the package nobody bid for. The final award was 8000.

Andhra Pradesh agreed to buy 7,000 MW in 2021 after the tariff was cut to Rs 2.42 a unit. An objector told the Commission the ceiling tariff had been raised to accommodate the manufacturing condition; the Commission declined to examine the bidding structure and adopted the tariffs. This is the transaction behind the 2024 New York indictment, whose allegations are reported in Part 3 as allegations.
