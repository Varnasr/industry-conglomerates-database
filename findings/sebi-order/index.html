SEBI found the offshore-fund allegation "not established", and said what its evidence did not cover

SEBI's final order of 28 September 2026 decided the central question raised in January 2023: whether offshore funds holding Adani shares were controlled by the founders' side, which would have breached the rule that a quarter of a listed company's shares be held by the public. It found the allegation not established, because there was no evidence that Vinod Adani directed the funds' investments.

The order adds that there was no allegation that he financed the investments, and that the result could have been different if there had been. Two individuals linked to the funds were fined Rs 20 lakh each for incorrect or incomplete information. The companies, Gautam Adani and 13 directors settled for Rs 1.48 crore without admission.
