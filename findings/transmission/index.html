Findings · Transmission · Part 1, Concentration

Adani is building or running 27 of 176 competitively bid transmission projects, and owns under 4% of the grid

The Central Electricity Authority's list of inter-state transmission projects awarded through tariff-based competitive bidding names Adani as the executing company on 27 of 176 projects commissioned or under construction, 15.3% by count.

Adani Energy Solutions' operational network was 1.5% of India's lines at 220 kV and above in 2015-16 and 3.8% in 2025-26. In the CEA's count, lines built by private companies and joint ventures of every owner were 9.0% of the network in 2025-26; the rest belongs to central and state government utilities.

How this was measured

Project counts are from the CEA's September 2026 status report on competitively bid projects; they are counts, not kilometres or cost, and name the current executing company, so a project Adani bought counts as Adani's. One project lists its company only as 'Reliance', which the report does not identify, and is not attributed to Reliance Industries. Network shares are Adani's operational circuit kilometres over the CEA's all-India total at 220 kV and above; Adani's network includes some lower-voltage lines, so the share is an upper bound.
