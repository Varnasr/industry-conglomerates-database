Where the information comes from

The 386 projects cite 753 sources on 136 websites. 383 projects cite at least one. Each source is listed beside its project on the projects page and in the map's pop-ups.

Most citations are the companies' own pages and filings, regulators such as the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board, and news reports. A company page shows what the company says it owns or runs. A regulator's record or a court document is stronger evidence where one exists.

Reading it fairly

  • A "government role" means a document shows one, such as a concession, a licence, a tender award, a state MoU, land from the state or public-sector finance. It is common in infrastructure and is not an allegation of wrongdoing.
  • Where nothing documented a role, the row says "No documented government role". That describes the documents found. A role may still exist in a document nobody has published.
  • Holdings through private equity and offshore companies are covered only in part.
  • This is independent research. It is not affiliated with either group or with any government. Corrections are welcome on GitHub.
